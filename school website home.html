Welcome to the website of BeaconHouse school

  • Origins: It began as Les Anges Montessori Academy with just 19 toddlers and was established by Mrs. Nasreen Mahmud Kasuri.
  • Global Presence: The network serves over 380,000 students globally and operates across several countries, including Pakistan, the UAE, Oman, Malaysia, Thailand, the Philippines, and the UK.
  • Educational Ecosystem: It extends beyond K-12 schooling through affiliated institutions like Beaconhouse National University, Concordia Colleges, and Beaconhouse International College.

The Beaconhouse School System is one of the largest private school networks in the world. Tracing its roots back to the Les Anges Montessori Academy founded in Lahore in 1975, it has expanded into the UK, Malaysia, Thailand, the Philippines, the UAE, Oman, and Belgium.
